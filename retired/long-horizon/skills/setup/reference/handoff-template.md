# Handoff template

Keep at most 80 lines by default. Small batches may update one continuation line.
Standing policy belongs in the overlay. Move detailed findings into durable files.
No credentials or live agent mappings; durable delegation result IDs are allowed.

```markdown
# Handoff
Next: 1.1 Establish fixture oracle; preparation is pending, no acceptance claimed.

## Blockers
- [1.1] Missing native bytes; recover: docs/capture.md; 2026-10-11

## Pending decisions
- Choose the reference corpus.

## Context
- [Current design](design.md)

## Parked

## Overrides
```

During work write findings with observation/checked/cited class, domain, uncertainty
and a durable recovery route. Capture mid-batch ideas under Parked without changing
milestone scope. A requested override is one line `Override: what; why; what remains`.
For explicit pause preserve dirty work and precise next steps; do not integrate
unless authorized. After a merge conflict regenerate the shared current section
from goals, reachable evidence and remaining closure; preserve durable history.
